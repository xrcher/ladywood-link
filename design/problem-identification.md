Quoting from the case itself it has stated that ‘ one in every five homes are likely to be at risk by 2100’ and one of the main issues was that raw sewage has been released into the waterways at least 534,000 times. Second of all we chose to take the problem of wastage. One of the main problems that the locals face is the fact that there is a lot of waste that piles up which causes pollution issues such as foul odour and this could lead to waste being destroyed and found on the streets by scavengers. A key factor that Ladieswood faces as well in terms of waste is the way in which each waste is separated. Through the case given, we have been able to identify that the locals tend to sometimes contaminate the wastage which leads to issues inculding the fact that some of the recycled waste cannot be recycled.
